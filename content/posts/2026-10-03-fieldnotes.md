Security work leaves behind more than a final report. There are assumptions to question, unfamiliar mechanisms to understand, and small details that change how a system behaves.

This journal is a home for those details.

## What belongs here

**Articles** explore smart contract security concepts and research methods. **Report writeups** put selected findings in context. **Hack analyses** trace incidents from the original assumption to the resulting failure.

The aim is to make each piece useful on its own: enough context to understand the system, a clear explanation of the failure, and a concrete lesson to carry into the next review.

## Start with the evidence

My [portfolio](https://p4y4b13.com/) collects selected work and links to the original results. Future technical writeups will live alongside these notes, with code, references, and reproductions where available.
